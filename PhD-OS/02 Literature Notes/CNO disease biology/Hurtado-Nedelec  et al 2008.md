The paper **Hurtado-Nedelec 2008** titled "_Characterization of the immune response in the synovitis, acne, pustulosis, hyperostosis, osteitis (SAPHO) syndrome_" sought to investigate the roles of innate and adaptive immunity in patients with SAPHO syndrome.

### **Objective: What the paper set out to do**

- **Immune Examination:** The primary goal was to examine both innate and adaptive immune responses in SAPHO syndrome to determine if it involves genetic, infectious, or immunological components.
- **Comparative Analysis:** Researchers aimed to compare SAPHO patients' immune profiles with those of individuals with **Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)** and **Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA)**.
- **Treatment Impact:** The study also set out to analyze the effect of the TNF-α antagonist **etanercept** on these immune parameters to better understand the role of TNF-α in the disease.

### **Methods: What the paper did**

- **Participant Groups:** The study involved **29 SAPHO patients**, 22 RA patients, 21 PsA patients, and 15 healthy controls.
- **Adaptive Immunity Testing:** Researchers assayed total serum immunoglobulins and screened for a wide range of autoantibodies (e.g., RF, anti-CCP, ANA, and ENA).
- **Innate Immunity Testing:** They quantified blood **polymorphonuclear neutrophil (PMN)** functions, including oxidative burst and the production of cytokines like IL-8, IL-18, and TNF-α.
- **Pathogen Response:** They tested the ex vivo PMN response to _**Propionibacterium acnes**_, a bacterium suspected of triggering the syndrome.
- **Clinical Follow-up:** Eight patients (including three with SAPHO) were tested before and after a 28-day course of etanercept therapy.

### **Results: What was reported**

- **Cytokine Profiles:** SAPHO syndrome was associated with **elevated plasma levels of IL-8 and IL-18**. Notably, IL-8 levels were significantly higher in patients specifically presenting with **anterior chest wall lesions**.
- **Neutrophil Hyperresponsiveness:** Purified PMNs from SAPHO patients were hyperresponsive, producing higher levels of IL-8 and TNF-α upon stimulation compared to healthy controls, though their **oxidative burst was normal**.
- **P. acnes Desensitization:** Interestingly, the PMN response to _P. acnes_ was **impaired** (hyporeactive) in SAPHO patients compared to the RA and PsA groups. This suggests a "desensitization" likely caused by chronic exposure to the bacterium.
- **Lack of Autoimmunity:** No circulating autoantibodies were detected in SAPHO patients, suggesting that **autoimmunity is not a primary driver** in the early stages of the disease.
- **Humoral Response:** Patients showed significantly increased circulating **IgA levels**, which researchers interpreted as evidence of an ongoing anti-infectious response.
- **Effect of Etanercept:** Treatment with etanercept down-regulated PMN activation (reducing IL-8 and TNF-α production) and significantly **lowered plasma RANKL levels**, while plasma TNF-α levels paradoxically increased (likely due to the drug acting as a decoy receptor).

### **Conclusion**

The study concludes that SAPHO syndrome is characterized by a strong humoral and cellular **pro-inflammatory response** that may be **triggered by an infectious state involving _P. acnes_**, rather than a primarily autoimmune mechanism.
