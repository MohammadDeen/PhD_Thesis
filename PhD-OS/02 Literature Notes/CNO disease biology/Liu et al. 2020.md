The paper **Liu et al. 2020**, titled “_Synovitis, acne, pustulosis, hyperostosis, and osteitis syndrome: review and update_,” provides a comprehensive overview of the current understanding and management of SAPHO syndrome.

### **Objective: What the paper set out to do**

- **Comprehensive Review:** The authors aimed to summarize and update the medical community on the **epidemiology, etiopathogenesis, and clinical manifestations** of SAPHO syndrome.
- **Clinical Guidance:** The study set out to discuss current diagnostic strategies, differential diagnoses, and the latest therapeutic options to help clinicians improve patient outcomes and prevent irreversible damage.

### **Methods: What the paper did**

- **Literature Synthesis:** The authors conducted a narrative review of recent research advances in the field.
- **Cohort Integration:** They integrated clinical data and observations from a large **Chinese cohort of 354 patients** to provide real-world statistics on symptom frequency and distribution.
- **Comparative Analysis:** The review compared various imaging techniques (X-ray, CT, MRI, WBBS, and PET/CT) and summarized potential laboratory biomarkers.
- **Treatment Summary:** The researchers tabulated current pharmacological interventions, categorizing them by drug class and reported patient response.

### **Results: What was reported**

- **Autoinflammatory Pathogenesis:** The paper emphasizes that SAPHO is an autoinflammatory disorder driven by an elevation of pro-inflammatory cytokines, including **TNF-α, IL-1, IL-8, IL-17, and IL-18**, as well as dysregulation of the **IL-23/Th17 axis**.
- **Infectious and Genetic Links:** It highlights _**P. acnes**_ as the most commonly isolated pathogen from lesions, potentially triggering the **NLRP3 inflammasome**. It also notes genetic susceptibility involving specific SNPs (e.g., _MDM2, p53, CDKAL1_) and mutations in the _NCSTN_ gene in sporadic cases.
- **Clinical Characteristics:** **Hyperostosis and sterile osteitis** are the core hallmarks, with the **anterior chest wall (ACW)** being the most characteristic site of involvement. Skin manifestations like **palmoplantar pustulosis (PPP)** and **severe acne (SA)** were present in the vast majority (94.6%) of the cited Chinese cohort.
- **Diagnostic Tools:** While diagnosis is often of exclusion, imaging remains critical. The review notes the importance of the **'Bull’s head' pattern** on whole-body bone scintigraphy (WBBS) and the ability of CT to detect early bone hyperplasia.
- **Therapeutic Landscape:**
    - **First-line:** NSAIDs for rapid pain relief.
    - **Second-line:** cDMARDs (like **methotrexate** or leflunomide) and **bisphosphonates** (especially pamidronate) for bone remodeling.
    - **Advanced options:** Targeted biologics (anti-TNF, IL-17, and IL-23 inhibitors) and small molecules like the **JAK inhibitor tofacitinib** show promise for refractory cases.

### **Conclusion**

The study concludes that SAPHO syndrome is a stable but heterogeneous clinical entity with a generally good long-term prognosis if managed early. Because the condition is often underdiagnosed or misdiagnosed, a **comprehensive evaluation** using multiple imaging modalities and a stratified treatment approach is essential to achieve disease control and improve quality of life.
