Hyperactivation of **IL-36 signaling** is established as a central driver of GPP.

- **Activation:** IL-36 cytokines (IL−36α,β,γ) are initially produced as inactive precursors that must be activated by **neutrophil-derived proteases** (such as elastase and proteinase 3).
- **Inflammatory Loop:** Once active, IL-36 induces keratinocytes to produce pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL−1β, IL-17A, TNF−α) and chemokines (CXCL1, CXCL8). This creates a chemokine gradient that recruits even more neutrophils to the epidermis, establishing a self-amplifying feedback loop.
- **Spatial Specificity:** This IL-36-dependent amplification primarily occurs within the **supraspinous layer** of the epidermis, which corresponds to where pustules typically form
