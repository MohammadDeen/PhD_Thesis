## 1. Disease biology

*18 papers · 12 open access*

  

SAPHO/CNO/CRMO clinical context and mechanism. **Now review-balanced:** the Hofmann trilogy, Brandt, Hurtado-Nedelec, Gao and Li supply the primary studies underneath claims previously carried by reviews alone. Cox and Assmann 2020 are the paired positive and negative FBLIM1 evidence.

  

**Hurtado-Nedelec 2008** — Characterization of the immune response in the synovitis, acne, pustulosis, hyperostosis, osteitis (SAPHO) syndrome  

*Rheumatology (Oxford)* · PMID [18559374](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18559374/) · [doi:10.1093/rheumatology/ken185](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/ken185) · 🔒 subscription  

> SAPHO-specific immune phenotype — neutrophil and cytokine characterisation in synovitis/acne/pustulosis/hyperostosis/osteitis. Primary evidence for the SAPHO arm of §0.1, previously carried by reviews alone

  

**Hofmann 2011 2011** — Chronic non-bacterial osteomyelitis is associated with impaired Sp1 signaling, reduced IL10 promoter phosphorylation, and reduced myeloid IL-10 expression  

*Clin Immunol* · PMID [21925952](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21925952/) · [doi:10.1016/j.clim.2011.08.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clim.2011.08.012) · 🔒 subscription  

> **Primary source for reduced regulatory cytokine output.** CNO monocytes show reduced IL-10 on LPS stimulation via impaired Sp1 signalling and reduced IL10 promoter phosphorylation. Underpins the 'reduced regulatory cytokines' claim in §0.1

  

**Hofmann 2012 2012** — Attenuated TLR4/MAPK signaling in monocytes from patients with CRMO results in impaired IL-10 expression  

*Clin Immunol* · PMID [22940633](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22940633/) · [doi:10.1016/j.clim.2012.07.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clim.2012.07.012) · 🔒 subscription  

> **The TLR4→ERK/MAPK defect.** Attenuated ERK1/2 signalling on TLR4 activation causes the failure to induce IL-10 in CRMO monocytes. Mechanistic link between innate stimulation and the cytokine imbalance — directly relevant to the priming step in §3.2

  

**Hofmann 2015 2015** — Altered expression of IL-10 family cytokines in monocytes from CRMO patients result in enhanced IL-1β expression and release  

*Clin Immunol* · PMID [26404542](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26404542/) · [doi:10.1016/j.clim.2015.09.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clim.2015.09.013) · 🔒 subscription  

> **Connects the IL-10 family defect to NLRP3.** Reduced IL-10/IL-19 with the NLRP3 inflammasome mediating IL-1β cleavage and release in CRMO monocytes. The closest human-disease precedent for the mechanism §0.3 names as under test

  

**Cox 2017** — Recessive coding and regulatory mutations in FBLIM1 underlie the pathogenesis of chronic recurrent multifocal osteomyelitis (CRMO)  

*PLoS One* · PMID [28301468](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28301468/) · [PMC5354242](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5354242/) · [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0169687](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0169687) · 🟢 PMC

**Brandt 2018** — CD14(+) monocytes contribute to inflammation in chronic nonbacterial osteomyelitis (CNO) through increased NLRP3 inflammasome expression

## Two findings that should change the aims document

  

**Gao 2021 reports that SAPHO serum extracellular vesicles *inhibit* osteoclastogenesis** (88 patients, 118 controls). The central hypothesis in §0.2 frames the loop as licensing osteoclastic osteolysis alongside hyperostosis. A circulating factor that suppresses osteoclastogenesis is not fatal to that model — SAPHO lesions are simultaneously lytic and sclerotic, and serum is not the lesional microenvironment — but the tension is real and a reviewer will find it. It should be addressed explicitly rather than left out.

  

**Li 2026 is the closest published parallel to the central hypothesis.** An SLC2A10 gain-of-function variant in a CNO family drives glycolytic macrophage polarisation, increased osteoclast differentiation and impaired osteoblast mineralisation — the same myeloid-to-bone loop structure, in the same disease, through a different gene. It is a single unreplicated family, so it is supporting precedent rather than evidence. It considerably strengthens the plausibility argument in §0.2.
