Multiple experiments using the same method. (12/2025, 01/2026)
4 × 10⁵ HEK293 cells / well in 2 mL well seeded the day before, 22 hrs, 75-80%  confluence achieved. Transfected with 2 µg total DNA from my vector pCMV and a pEGFP reporter vector in a 9:1 ratio using jet prime reagent and protocol for 6 well plates. Each condition was transfected in 3 replicates  .  After 24hrs of transfection, media is removed, washing with 1ml warm pbs, 1 ml DMEM +0.2% BSA was added and incubated for 1 hr. After that, stimulation with 5μM AA for 40 minutes. Supernatants were then collected, centrifuged to remove debris and stored in -80  freezer.
